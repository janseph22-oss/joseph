About ExcuseEase

ExcuseEase is an official digital absence management portal specifically designed for City College of Cagayan de Oro.

Our platform bridges the gap between students and faculty members by providing a hassle-free, paperless, and organized system for submitting and approving official excuse letters.

Key Benefits:

  • Fast and direct online submission to instructors.
  • Digital attachment of valid medical or personal excuse proofs.
  • Real-time tracking of submission status and history review.
  • Secure environment protecting student and teacher data privacy.

Sustainable Development Goals (SDG)

ExcuseEase supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) to promote better education and institutional sustainability:

  • SDG 4: Quality Education - Ensures inclusive and equitable quality education by minimizing classroom disruptions and maintaining clear communication regarding student attendance.
  • SDG 9: Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure - Fosters digital transformation within the academic institution through modernized administrative workflows.
  • SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production - Reduces paper consumption significantly by shifting traditional paper-based excuse slips into digital submissions.
